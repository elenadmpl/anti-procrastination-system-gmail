Your board, at your pace.

Pick a tool, then click the canvas to add a sticky, task or frame. Drag cards to arrange them; double-click to edit. Use Shift + click to select several cards, then drag or delete them together. Drag the bottom-right corner to resize.

Hold Space to pan. Ctrl + scroll zooms. Connect links two selected cards. Draw leaves a freehand sketch. Ctrl+Z undoes your last edit.

Keep notes about real conversations in People. Use the journal for ideas and daily reflections. The follow-up sprint provides a timer and an editable draft, with you choosing the next action.

Everything saves in this browser’s database. Export a private backup regularly. A hosted copy or another device has a separate database; use export/import to move your work.

This is an original Miro-style personal board. It supports the core canvas workflow; live team collaboration and automatic account sync are not included.
